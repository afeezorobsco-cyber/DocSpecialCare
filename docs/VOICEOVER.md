# DocSpecialCare: full voice-over (≈ 2 min 20 s)

Read at a relaxed pace. **[Brackets]** = what's on screen. *(pause)* = wait while the transaction confirms; keep recording, you can trim it later.

---

**[Landing page, slow scroll]**

> In many hospitals, seeing a specialist takes a whole day. You queue to see a doctor, queue again for the specialist, again at the lab, and again at the pharmacy. Referrals get lost, results are passed around on paper, and you pay upfront, even if the doctor never shows up.
>
> This is DocSpecialCare. It puts that whole journey online, and NEAR is the referee between the patient, the doctor, the lab and the pharmacy. It holds the money, records every hand-off, and decides who can open each medical record.

**[Click Try the demo → Patient: Amaka]**

> Let's follow Amaka. She has chest pains and needs to see a doctor.

**[Book Dr. Tunde Bello → pick a time → Pay 0.1 NEAR & book]**

> She picks a general physician and a time slot. No waiting room. She pays 0.1 NEAR, but it doesn't go to the doctor yet. It's locked in a NEAR smart contract, and only released when the consultation is done. If he doesn't show up, the contract refunds her. *(pause)*

**[Open consultation → type "I get chest pains when climbing stairs" → Send]**

> At her time slot she opens the consultation. The chat is end-to-end encrypted, and there's a private video call only the two of them can join.

**[Sign out → Dr. Tunde Bello → Open consultation (show her message) → close → Clinical actions → Refer → choose Dr. Ngozi Eze, type a short note → Send referral]**

> Dr. Tunde reads her message, and decides she needs a cardiologist. He writes a referral. That referral is recorded on NEAR: a specialist can't be booked without one, and only Amaka can use it. *(pause)*

**[Complete tab → Complete & get paid]**

> He completes the visit, and the contract releases his fee. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Patient → Specialist referrals → Book specialist → pick a time → pay]**

> Back to Amaka. Her referral is waiting, so she books the cardiologist directly. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Dr. Ngozi Eze → Clinical actions → Order test → CityLab → Order test]**

> Dr. Ngozi orders blood tests at a verified lab. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Patient → Lab tests → Pay 0.15 NEAR]**

> Amaka pays the lab, again into escrow. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → CityLab Diagnostics → Upload result → type a line + attach the report → Encrypt & submit]**

> The lab uploads her result. It's encrypted on the lab's computer before it ever leaves. NEAR only stores its fingerprint, and the list of who's allowed to open it. And the moment the result is submitted, the contract pays the lab. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Dr. Ngozi Eze → Lab orders → 🔒 Result → point at the green "Integrity verified" banner]**

> Dr. Ngozi opens the result. It's decrypted right here in her browser, and this green check proves it matches the fingerprint on NEAR, so nobody changed it after the lab sent it.

**[Close → Clinical actions → Prescribe → Atorvastatin, 20mg, once nightly, 30 days → Sign & issue prescription]**

> She prescribes medication. The prescription is signed on NEAR by a verified doctor, with an expiry date. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Patient → Prescriptions → choose HealthPlus Pharmacy → Send to pharmacy]**

> Amaka chooses where to collect it. Only that pharmacy can now read her prescription. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → HealthPlus Pharmacy → 🔒 View → type 0.05 → Send quote]**

> The pharmacy sees exactly what was prescribed, and sends a price. *(pause)*

**[Sign out → Patient → Pay 0.05 NEAR → Sign out → Pharmacy → Dispense & receive]**

> She pays into escrow, and the pharmacy dispenses. That prescription can only ever be dispensed once. *(pause)*

**[Verify a prescription → type 1 → Check → shows "Dispensed"]**

> Any pharmacy can check a prescription against NEAR: who issued it, whether it's expired, and whether it was already filled. No forged prescriptions, and no double filling.

**[Switch to NearBlocks tab → scroll the transaction list → click the book_consultation row → point at Deposit 0.1 NEAR]**

> Every step you just saw was a real transaction on NEAR testnet: booking, referral, lab result, prescription, dispensing. Here's the 0.1 NEAR fee going into escrow.
>
> Why NEAR? Transactions confirm in about a second and cost a tiny fraction of a cent, so paying per consultation actually works. And accounts have readable names, so it's clear who's the doctor, the lab or the pharmacy.

**[Back to the landing page]**

> Next, we're adding stablecoin payments, and gas-free transactions, so patients never need to hold crypto.
>

## 60-second version (if you want it short)

> Seeing a specialist can take a whole day of queues. DocSpecialCare puts that journey online, with NEAR as the referee.
>
> **[Patient books + pays]** Amaka books a doctor and pays into a NEAR smart contract. The doctor is only paid when the visit is done.
>
> **[GP refers + completes]** He refers her to a cardiologist. The referral lives on NEAR, and only she can use it.
>

>
> **[Specialist: Integrity verified → Prescribe]** The specialist decrypts it, proves it wasn't altered, and prescribes.
>
> **[Pharmacy dispenses → Verify shows Dispensed]** The pharmacy dispenses once, and anyone can verify it.
>
> **[NearBlocks]** Every step was a real NEAR transaction: fast, and cheap enough to pay per consultation. DocSpecialCare: care without the queue.
